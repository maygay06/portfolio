Maylin Aregger
I move between lines, light, and motion. As a graphic designer, I think in images that aim to tell a story beyond what meets the eye.
Born and raised in Lucerne, Switzerland (2006), I am currently training as a graphic designer at the Fachklasse Grafik Luzern. My daily life is my most vital source of inspiration: paths through the city, fleeting observations, sounds, textures, and moods that often go unnoticed. I collect these impressions and translate them into visual concepts.
My work focuses on systems and movement. I am fascinated by how design functions between order and chaos, and how personal observations evolve into visual concepts that translate spaces, sounds, or memories.
To me, design is a process: an act of looking closely, experimenting, and thinking forward. My goal is not just a finished image, but a visual experience that lingers a moment longer.
