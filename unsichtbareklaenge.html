INVISIBLE SOUNDS
How can music be told without the act of listening? Inspired by experimental notation systems such as those by Marco Fusinato, I created a visual translation of sound. Through the interplay of typography and motion, a graphic language emerged — one that makes rhythm, intensity, and structure visible. My aim was to develop a system that doesn’t just depict music but lets it be felt: a visual soundscape poised between design and composition.
